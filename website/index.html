Everything you need

A real editor first.
Then the magic.

Tandem keeps the workflow you already know. No node graphs, no subscriptions, no compromises on speed.

Professional timeline workflow

Scrub tracks with audio, ripple delete, compound clips, magnet snapping, copy/paste properties and clips between projects and windows. Clip speed from 1% to 10,000% and unlimited undo with a jumpable history.

Fast playback & scrubbing

Timeline-wide frame cache, optional background proxies and up to 8× playback with pitch-preserved audio.

1×2×4×8×

Hardware accelerated

Decode on NVDEC, Vulkan Video or VideoToolbox. Composite on the GPU with Vulkan or Metal. Export on NVENC, Vulkan or VideoToolbox.

NVDECVulkanMetalNVENC

Audio, finished in place

A mixer with per-track and master gain, balance, meters and an effect chain: 6-band EQ and 3-band multiband compressor over the live spectrum, normalization (sample peak, true peak or EBU R128), VST3 plugins. Record voiceover straight onto the timeline.

Color correction

Shadows, midtones, highlights and offset wheels, auto balance and GPU scopes: waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope, histogram.

Keyframes done right

Crop, zoom, rotation, position, speed, opacity, gain: every parameter keyframable, with a dedicated curve editor.

One library, every project

Media, compound clips and property presets (a 2× punch-in, a drop shadow, a voice level) applied with a drag or a shortcut.

Titles, filters, transitions

Titles, solid colors, filters and transitions applied straight from the timeline. No node graph to wire up.

AI Infographics

Drivable by AI agents

A built-in MCP server lets an agent like Claude Code import, cut, measure audio, look at frames and export, headless or in the window you're editing. Every agent call is its own undo step.
